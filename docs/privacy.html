COMFORTABLY LOCAL

Privacy

Your audio stays on your Mac. Chanterelle processes captured system playback locally, in memory.

The current app does not save audio recordings, send audio over a network, include analytics, or use microphone or screen capture APIs.

System audio permission

Capture starts only after you click Start tuning. Every launch begins with tuning off. System playback capture can include sound from other running apps. The tap excludes Chanterelle’s own playback; output goes to your selected physical device.

Local preferences

The app stores tone controls, selected output device identifier, and one custom preset in local macOS preferences. Flat EQ resets active tone and trim, but keeps the saved preset and output preference. It does not erase local data or revoke macOS permission.

Diagnostics and reports

Explicit diagnostics can contain device names, identifiers and local paths. Review them before sharing a bug report. Test outputs are developer files, not automatic recordings. macOS manages permission records in Privacy & Security.

This website

The site uses local CSS, SVG and an app screenshot, system fonts, and native HTML controls. It includes no JavaScript, trackers, remote fonts, CDN, or embedded services. GitHub Pages and your browser may make network requests and keep their own access logs. External GitHub links follow GitHub’s policies.

Scope

These statements describe this repository’s source. Modified or third-party binaries may behave differently. Read the source privacy document.
