# Possible next steps

These are ideas for later review, not shipped features or commitments.

- Named preset collections, import/export with a small validated local format, and a dedicated reset-all-data control.
- More accessible input and output metering, peak hold and clipping history, plus clearer route and capture recovery guidance.
- An optional frequency response preview generated from the actual DSP coefficients, clearly distinguished from speaker calibration.
- User-controlled per-output preferences after live disconnect, sleep and permission recovery tests establish safe behavior.
- Measured speaker correction only with documented hardware measurements and model matching.
- CI on supported Apple hardware and broader live hardware acceptance, including live process taps under hardened runtime.

Future validation includes live listening acceptance of 1.1.0 at comfortable volume, including normal quit, output disconnect, wake and permission recovery. No extra drivers, automatic route/volume changes, or network service are planned for this pass.
