A LITTLE HELP GETTING SETTLED

Support

Start with a familiar track, a comfortable volume and the Neutral preset. Small adjustments are a good place to begin.

The usage guide explains the controls. The build guide covers building and installing from source.

Download and open

Get Chanterelle 1.1.0 for Apple silicon and the SHA-256 checksums. Requires macOS 14.2 or later. Extract the ZIP and read INSTALL.md, then open Chanterelle.app.

The release is Developer ID signed and Apple notarized. Open normally and confirm macOS’s first-open prompt. System audio capture permission is a separate approval when you start tuning. Default source builds are ad hoc signed. See the release notes for distribution details.

Silent or waiting for audio?

  • Stop tuning and confirm ordinary playback works.
  • Select the same stereo physical output in macOS Sound Settings and Chanterelle. It must already be your Mac’s default output.
  • Confirm system audio capture permission in Privacy & Security. Quit and reopen the app after permission changes.
  • After an output change or sleep, confirm your route and restart tuning manually.

Some protected or directly routed audio may not be captured. Capture and playback also need matching nominal sample rates.

Report a bug or suggest a feature

Open a GitHub issue with your macOS and app version, output model and connection, steps to reproduce, expected result, and observed status. Review diagnostics for device identifiers and local paths before sharing. Do not upload recordings or credentials.

Security reports

Use private vulnerability reporting for sensitive reports. The security document describes the project’s reporting and distribution practices.

Compatibility and validation

Chanterelle 1.1.0, build 5, targets Apple silicon (arm64) and macOS 14.2+. Automated build, DSP and off-state checks do not establish live audio quality, latency, permission or recovery behavior. Hardware acceptance remains necessary. Read the full limits and validation evidence.
